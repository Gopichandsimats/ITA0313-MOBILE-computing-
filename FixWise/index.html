DEVICE WELLNESS

Small habits. Fewer faults.

Practical care reminders for common devices.

▣

Smartphone care

  • Use a certified charger and undamaged cable.
  • Keep ports dry and free of dust.
  • Install security and system updates.
  • Avoid prolonged heat and direct sunlight.
  • Back up important data regularly.
▰

Laptop & PC care

  • Keep vents clear; use on a hard surface.
  • Back up files and update software.
  • Use surge protection where appropriate.
  • Keep liquids away from electronics.
  • Shut down before connecting internal parts.
⌂

Home appliances

  • Follow the manufacturer's load limits.
  • Keep appliances ventilated and clean.
  • Inspect plugs and cables for damage.
  • Unplug before external cleaning.
  • Use a qualified technician for repairs.
⌁

Network & Wi-Fi

  • Place the router in an open central area.
  • Use a strong, unique Wi-Fi password.
  • Restart equipment only when appropriate.
  • Keep router firmware up to date.
  • Check provider outages before resetting.
RememberDo not open power supplies, batteries, microwave ovens, or other high-voltage components. Repairs involving mains electricity should be handled by qualified professionals.
